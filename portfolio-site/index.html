( About me )

I’m a Johannesburg-based senior designer, illustrator and art director. My practice moves between brand identity, editorial and annual reports, digital design, illustration, storyboarding and integrated campaign rollout.

My work is grounded in African visual culture and a love of useful systems. I turn complex ideas into visual languages that can travel across channels, audiences and formats.
