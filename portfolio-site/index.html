About

A short introduction

I’m Musonda Kabwe, a senior designer, illustrator and art director based in Johannesburg. I work across brand identity, digital and social design, illustration, storyboarding and integrated campaign rollout.

My practice is grounded in African visual culture and a love of clear, useful systems. I help teams turn complex ideas into visual languages that can travel across channels, audiences and formats.
