( About me )

I’m a Johannesburg-based senior designer, illustrator and art director. My practice focuses on brand identity, art direction, typography, visual language and rollout systems.

My work is grounded in African visual culture and a love of useful systems. I turn strategy into visual identities that can travel across channels, audiences and formats.
